Welcome to the Roebling Labs Blog

We're excited to launch the Roebling Labs blog. This is where we'll share updates on our vessel collision warning systems, insights from bridge safety research, and news about our deployments across the country.

Why We're Starting This Blog

The collapse of the Francis Scott Key Bridge on March 26, 2024 was a wake-up call for the bridge industry. When the containership Dali lost power and collided with the bridge, police had only two minutes to close the roadway. Six people lost their lives, and the economic impact reached an estimated $5 billion.

In November 2025, the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) identified 68 bridges across the United States that are potentially vulnerable to vessel collision risk. They recommended that bridge owners evaluate the need for motorist warning systems capable of activating when a threat is identified.

At Roebling Labs, we've been working to develop exactly these kinds of systems. Through this blog, we want to share what we're learning and help bridge owners understand the technology available to protect the traveling public.

What You'll Find Here

In future posts, we'll cover topics including:

  • How AIS (Automatic Identification System) tracking works for vessel collision warning
  • The role of computer vision in enhancing bridge safety systems
  • Case studies from our prototype deployments
  • Updates on NTSB recommendations and industry standards
  • Technical deep-dives into threat assessment algorithms
